How to care for our stream.

To maintain our streams ongoing health, we need to take care of it properly. Meaning, cleaning up after ourselves, not littering and keeping the stream clean. In addition, planting near the bank helps the bank maintain it's integrity and not collapse entirely. The streams health is important to our local ecosystem, so maintain a clean enviroment otherwise litter may get in the stream and affect wildlife within the stream.
