Information on the environment group.

The environment group is a group of around 100+ students who contribute to their local communinities and others by lookings after, and taking care of our ecosystems. Their recent activities include; placing traps for pests like rats who endanger local wildlife, plant trees and srubs to assist with our ecosystems and to maintain the ongoing health of our local stream.
